[_About Feeds._](https://aboutfeeds.com/)

Feeds work in any reader; you don't need a Facebook™ or Twitter (𝕏) to access them. Better readers would make social following part of the default Internet, and survive any company's death -- you keep your 'timeline' by copying your feed list to a new reader. Your friends don't have to be on the same platform.

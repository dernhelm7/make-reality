
Hypnosis for Sleep

*Hypnos, in Greek mythology, is the god of sleep.*

Hypnosis is not sleep, but if you do it while lying down you are liable to fall asleep. If you want to use it for therapy or other things you’ll do better to sit up.

What hypnosis is: a more suggestable, relaxed state. You can use that state in lots of ways; it’s a bit like having sudo access to your brain. A lot of hypnotherapy is essentially the process of installing TAPs [[Making intentions concrete - Trigger-Action Planning — LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/v4nNuJBZWPkMkgQRb/making-intentions-concrete-trigger-action-planning)] while you’re in sudo mode. Pain management uses the admin access of the brain to get the brain to turn down/off some pain signals (apparently it may also reduce blood loss when used during surgery/dentistry, etc. [THE USE OF HYPNOSIS IN EMERGENCY MEDICINE - ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0733862705701280)).

➜ All hypnosis is self-hypnosis.

Someone else can guide you or you can guide yourself -- either way you ultimately are in control and can ‘let’ yourself enter the suggested state…or not. Some people are better at ‘being hypnotized’ than others; it’s normally a skill you can get better at.

A good imagination seems to correlate with being good at being hypnotized -- if you easily get lost in a book or stay focused on an imaginary scenario, it’s a similar mental state.

## How-to

These are steps you can take yourself through to put yourself in a hypnotic trance. If you’re using this for sleep don’t feel bound to finish the process. If you’re falling asleep, just fall!

1. Relax. Find a comfortable position, and try to notice tension…and let it go. Legs, arms, toes, fingers, face, shoulders, neck… (keep letting go of tension whenever you notice it throughout the process)  

2. Think of a Quiet Place you’ve been or read about or imagined where you feel the most safe and calm, a place where you can let go of worries and responsibilities. Picture it in as much detail as you can.  

3. Build a staircase in your mind; ten steps leading down into your place. Tell yourself that the deeper down you go the more comfortable and the more relaxed you will become.  

4. Take the first step slowly. Notice how the ground feels under each foot, touch the wall or railing of the staircase with your hand, notice the perspective shift as you descend, the smell of the air around you. As you step down you relax a little more, your eyes feel heavier, sleepier. **Keep letting go of tension whenever you notice it.**
   
   Take the second step, noticing awareness slipping away, sinking deeper into calm,  
   With each step down you feel more comfortable and relaxed.  
   
   Take the third step…  
   
   Guide yourself slowly down, taking each step slowly, letting yourself settle in more and more with each step, and let sleep come whenever it comes.

Step # 2 - 4 are really just: sink into a trance. For this exercise I used one that works well for me, but there are many ways to do this! You can google self hypnosis induction and find many ideas. One might fit you perfectly; you can also make one up to fit you and your purpose for the session. For sleep, some other ideas are to imagine you’re covered by a heavy blanket and it gets heavier, and heavier, … or to imagine sinking into a soft material that holds you still.  

Whatever induction you do don’t hesitate to be repetitive; suggest things over and over until your brain lets it happen; don’t worry if it feels a little absurd or like it ‘doesn’t work’; it’s like practicing a skill over and over, except the ‘action’ is generated in your brain, with words.  

## Hypnotherapy

Once you are in your Quiet Place, or in a trance via some other induction, you can just go to sleep. You can also give yourself suggestions to help you later. This is mostly what hypnotherapy is -- forming these suggestions well. Think of it as prompt engineering for your subconscious. Most of the work is pre-planning. Identifying the problem you want to solve and coming up with the suggestion that will target it effectively; the last, ‘easy’ bit is getting into the trance and affirming the new behavior (or whatever it is).

Sleep-related suggestions you could try: 

- Every time I enter hypnosis I relax more deeply
- Whenever I sleep I sleep deeply, fully
- If I wake up I can close my eyes and sink back into deep sleep
